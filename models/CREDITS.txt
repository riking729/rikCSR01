asset with no wheels, so it was deliberately not included.

Changes made for the game:
  - Imported in Godot 4 as GLB (embedded materials, external textures kept).
  - Wheels are separate nodes (<model>_wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr) and are rotated
    directly by scripts/wheels.gd. The Tuk Tuk has three wheels
    (_wheel_f, _wheel_rl, _wheel_rr); the Hover Car has no wheels.
    The Race Car names them _wheel_front_l/front_r/rear_l/rear_r.
  - Scene scale/offset: none needed (models are in metres, centred at the
    origin, resting at y=0, front pointing to +Z like the other cars).
  - No model was modified; materials and textures are the originals.

All models are generic vehicles: they carry no manufacturer badges,
logos or trademarks.
